About

I'm Tromaine Jones, a multidisciplinary graphic and motion designer focused on building visual experiences that move people.

My work sits at the intersection of sport, culture, entertainment, and technology. I love turning raw energy into sharp visuals, whether that means a motion-first campaign, a brand system, or a 3D-driven concept that helps a story feel bigger than the screen.

I approach design with a strong eye for storytelling, typography, pacing, and atmosphere. The goal is always the same: create work that feels intentional, cinematic, and unforgettable.

Whether I'm collaborating with athletes, teams, or brands, I bring a sports-driven mindset and a design process rooted in clarity, emotion, and momentum.
